FCMS Prototype - Final Requirements
Version: Final prototype permission model

1. 18 Pradeshikams.
2. Main Committee can view all Pradeshikams.
3. Main Committee can select Pradeshikam when adding a member.
4. Pradeshikam users are locked to their own Pradeshikam.
5. One member can have multiple receipts/payments.
6. Male age 21+ required amount: ₹8,000.
7. Female age 21+ required amount: ₹2,000.
8. Green: 100% paid; Yellow: 80%-99%; Red: below 80%.
9. Pradeshikam can add/delete members.
10. Pradeshikam can add payments but cannot edit/delete submitted payments.
11. Main Committee can edit/delete submitted payments.
12. Activity History is visible only to Main Committee.
13. Deleted/edited payment actions remain in audit history.
14. No Expected Collection.
15. CSV export.
